Project: Phishing Email Analysis and Incident Report

## Sample Information

- Type: Fictional training email
- Subject: Urgent: Verify Your Account

## Findings
1. The From address claims to represent an account security team.
2. The Reply-To address uses a different domain.
3. SPF is shown as fail in the illustrative Authentication-Results header.
4. DKIM is shown as fail in the illustrative Authentication-Results header.
5. DMARC is shown as fail in the illustrative Authentication-Results header.
6. The subject and message body use urgency to encourage account verification.

## Preliminary Assessment
The sample contains several suspicious characteristics and should be treated as a phishing-style training example.

Note: The email and authentication results are fictional. No live sender or domain verification has been performed.
